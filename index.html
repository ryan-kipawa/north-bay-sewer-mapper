North Bay Sewer Mapper

Find storm & sanitary sewers, build a SWMM model

Automatic draft model

Zoom to a neighbourhood (up to 2.5 km across) and press Infer sewer network. The app pulls streets and buildings from OpenStreetMap and bare-earth lidar. It then routes gravity sewers to outfalls and the treatment plant, sizes pipes with City design flows, sets inverts, draws subcatchments, and stitches construction-season imagery. Every pipe gets a probability. Work down the review queue, then calibrate against City drawings.

City plan & profile drawings

North Bay publishes engineering plan & profile drawings by street. They show maintenance hole positions, pipe sizes, materials and inverts — the best source for a SWMM model.

Export

Coordinates are NAD83 / UTM 17N metres, ready for SWMM's map. Flow units L/s. Unknown inverts are estimated from minimum cover and slope and flagged in comments.

Uncertainty

Sample the inferred model's uncertainty into many SWMM files: pipe sizes, inverts, imperviousness and widths vary, while checked pipes stay fixed. Run them all to see which results hold.

Design criteria

Defaults from the City's Engineering Design Guidelines (2021) where it gives a value; others are common SWMM starting values. Used for checks and estimates.
